# web/: aguagdl.com, plain HTML with no framework

- **Hosting.** Cloudflare Pages deploys `web/` on every push to `main` (output directory `web`). Its build command runs the pipelines' `--check`s (see `scripts/check.sh`), so a broken or stale data file stops the deploy. Pages serves `privacidad.html` at `/privacidad`. Unknown paths get an empty 404 (checked 22 Oct 2026), so `404.html` (`noindex`) only shows at /404 until Cloudflare's not-found handling is set to serve it. `www.aguagdl.com` 301-redirects to the apex through a Cloudflare Redirect Rule (proxied AAAA `www` → `100::`), not through Pages, so don't add `www` as a Pages custom domain. Cloudflare Web Analytics is enabled from the Pages project's Metrics tab; point 7 of the privacy notice describes it.
- **Every page** loads `/theme.js` in its `<head>`: the light/dark button. Light is the default, and a page's `prefers-color-scheme: dark` rules apply only after a visitor picks dark, so style dark mode only with those media queries and use `aguaDark()` instead of `matchMedia` in scripts; a switch fires a `tema` event on `document`, on which the maps recolour. Each page has one `<meta name="theme-color" content="#ffffff">`, without `media` (theme.js changes it). The icon sprite is referenced as `/icons.svg?v=N#name` and cached for a day, so bump `v` in every page when you change `icons.svg`. Keep the `facebook-domain-verification` meta tag. The pages that call the API (`index`, `reportes`, `mapa`, `avisos`) use `https://api.aguagdl.com`, and `?api=http://localhost:PORT` points them at a local one (only localhost or 127.0.0.1 is accepted, and only on the local preview: production's CSP blocks it).
- **`_headers`**: HSTS, nosniff, referrer and permissions policies (`/avisos` needs `geolocation=(self)`), framing only by aguagdl.com itself, and a CSP that allows only what the pages use: the API, OSM tiles, the players /noticias opens and Cloudflare's analytics beacon. Read the file for cache lifetimes; `/vendor/*` is immutable, so a new Leaflet goes in a new folder.
- **SEO.** `sitemap.xml` lists the indexable pages; `robots.txt` allows all. `llms.txt` tells AI agents how to use the API and data; keep it in step with the API. /noticias, /reportes, /exige and /avisos carry 1200×630 og:image cards (`img/og-*.jpg`); the other pages keep the 512 px icon.
- **`_redirects`** holds the 301s for old paths and retired variants, and `/reportes-portada`, a rewrite (200) of /reportes for the landing's frame. The dev server applies it.
- **The menu** has seven entries: «¿Hay agua?» (/reportes), «Exige» (/exige), «Avisos» (/avisos, the zone map), «Noticias», «Cuentas» (/promesas), «Contaminación» and «Antisionismo». They wrap into rows of four and three at 760 px or less, and below 980 px the icons hide.
- **Check visually** at phone and desktop width before pushing: `npm run dev`, then `node scripts/shot.mjs /route` (390 and 1300 px).
- `index.html`, the landing, in a newspaper style. Under the kicker «El agua es un derecho. Exígelo.» and the headline, with the illustration beside them (`img/ilustracion.png`, a caricature of Lemus cropped from `aguagdl_logo.png`; /noticias and /contamina show it beside their intro too), /reportes itself fills a frame edge to edge (88 % of the window's height, 80 % on phones): the whole city, its summary and its timeline, which waits on the first day until most of the frame is on screen and then plays once; three seconds after landing the page scrolls down to the frame unless the visitor has scrolled, touched anything or asked for less motion; then SIAPA's promises beside the CEA's own counts, «¿Y en tu colonia?», «Lo que se publica», the /exige and rivers boxes and the section asking Lemus and the Congreso to clean the rivers. Quotes appear as written, each linked; private people appear by colonia and date only, never by name. Nothing on it is about the rotation's schedule, zones or reminders: those live on /avisos, /mapa and the bot.
  - On phones (760 px or less) the landing is different: the frame isn't loaded; under the headline, swipeable cards with a still Leaflet map of each SIAPA zone (its outline, its colonias with publications tinted and badged by their count of publications, zoomed onto them, opening on zone 3, with how many of the zone's colonias have gone a week or more without water at its foot), then cards with the count, SIAPA's promise against the longest outage a news outlet reports, colonias by municipio and the report button; no auto-scroll (`data-quieto`).
  - The frame loads `/reportes-portada` (`noindex`), so Web Analytics doesn't count the landing's visits as visits to /reportes. Framed, /reportes shows only its map from the first paint (the `en-portada` class), opens its links in the whole window and posts `mapa-listo` to the landing once its map is drawn or has failed; the landing shows the frame then (its `load` event is the fallback) and passes its theme on. One finger on a touch screen and a plain wheel scroll the landing; two fingers, Ctrl/⌘ + wheel or a trackpad pinch move and zoom the map, and a hint says so.
- `reportes.html` (/reportes), edited directly (no generator): a full-screen map under the header, then the list of colonias, the week, the bot's answers by zone and «Cómo funciona». A timeline under the map (play, a stacked chart by day that doubles as the scrubber, `?dia=YYYY-MM-DD` opens a date; hidden in the landing's frame) lights colonias up on their first publication and shows the map's own reports by colonia and day for the last week, each report staying visible up to 3 days after its day (grey and dashed). A colonia clicked from a past day keeps that day in its card above the current 24/72-hour counts. Publication badges count only publications; anonymous current reports have separate donuts. The summary at the top right gives the colonias with a week or more, the municipio shares and the four colonias with the most reports, all following the timeline's day. It draws from the static files without waiting for the API: `/v1/reportes` is applied when it arrives, and the refresh every 2 minutes replaces only the open card's counts. On phones a colonia opens in a carousel card.
  - Current reports (24 hours) colour a colonia by strict majority, with an explicit mixed state when no answer has one. With no current reports, the previous 24–72 hours make the outline neutral and dashed, but a published colonia keeps its publication fill beneath at reduced opacity; silence never means the water returned. Publication history has the fill to itself only when there are no reports at all (one red ramp: 1–2, 3–6, 7–13, 14 or more; dashed when none gives days; dark mode inverts it); in the live latest-day view it greys out 7 days after the latest publication. Sources count once per account, page or group (each comment is its own). Eco is shown but never treated as proof.
  - A colonia's card leads with the latest map report and its time, then the current or stale counts, then its sources' images and posts (visual protest coverage first, then news and newest), and the report form; one «con agua» does not mark the whole colonia as recovered. `impactOf` prioritizes a publication only when `bloqueo` is true and it has a local `vista`, `captura`, or parent-post `en.vista`; it changes ordering, never which evidence is included. After a report it shows its link to share and /exige. A browser keeps its own report of a colonia for an hour in `localStorage`. If `publicaciones.json` doesn't load, the page says so.
  - `?q=&m=&cp=` opens a colonia; a bare `?q=` opens one only when one matches and lists them otherwise; `&reportar=1` opens the form.
- `avisos.html` (/avisos; /mizona and /llego redirect here) opens onto a full-screen map of SIAPA's colonias coloured by zone (a switch, or `?color=dias`, colours them by days without water) with the report badges on top, a strip of zone tiles (cut days, next cut, «Hoy hay corte»; a tap singles the zone out) and a finder: colonia search, «Buscar» and «Usar mi ubicación». A colonia's card gives its zone(s) in `mapa.js`'s wording, Telegram (`/start zN`) and the zone's calendar; `?q=&m=&cp=` and `?zona=` open one; a tap on a spot with no colonia asks `/v1/zone`. `mapa.html` (/mapa) shows every colonia filled by zone, with search; the days come from `/v1/zones`, never from the map file.
- `mapa.js` holds the map code /mapa, /avisos and /reportes share (TopoJSON decoding, zone colours, drawing, highlights, and the wording helpers `zones`, `label` and `describe`). Leaflet is self-hosted in `vendor/leaflet-1.9.4/`, with OSM tiles. `p` in the data is each multi-zone colonia's share of area per zone; below `BORDER` (15 %) the pages say the colonia is in one zone «al límite con» the other.
- `capas.html` (/capas, `noindex`, not in the menu yet): INEGI relief, 100 m contours and streams over SIAPA's colonias, each a toggle; the layers live in `web/capas/` and load only when switched on. Sources: CEM 3.0 at 30 m for Jalisco and Red Hidrográfica 1:50 000 ed. 2.0, RH12E, both under INEGI's free-use terms with credit (CEM: `elevacionesmex/api/archivo?version=1&opcion=30&clave=14`; RH12E: UPC 702825006861). Cut to the colonias' extent (−103.66, 20.33, −102.86, 21.0) with GDAL by hand: `gdalwarp -t_srs EPSG:3857` then `gdaldem hillshade -multidirectional -z 1.5` and `gdaldem color-relief` (green 700–1,450 m, sand 1,550–1,650 m, brown to 2,300 m, pale above) multiplied together (colour × (0.45 + 0.55 × shade)) → `relieve.jpg` (1600 px; the page's `BOUNDS` are its corners, its legend follows the ramp), `gdal_contour -i 100` on the model averaged to 0.0008° → `curvas.json`, and streams of Strahler order 2 or more, perennial ones and canals → `rios.json` (`t`, `o`). IMEPLAN's layers stay off the site until IMEPLAN grants a licence.
- `simple.html` is the generated low-data, no-JavaScript page (`pipelines/simple_page.py`). `transparencia.html`, `correcciones.html` and `participa.html` publish accountability, corrections and nonpartisan participation. `privacidad.html` and `terminos.html` are the privacy notice and terms; Meta's app settings link to them, so keep their URLs.
- Removed until there are enough reports, in git history: `resultados.html` (with `GET /v1/results`, `store.Results`).

## Brand assets (repo root)

The logo is a blue water drop (`#017CDC`, shadow `#015198`) with "GDL" in white. `aguagdl.svg` is for anything drawn in a page; `web/logo.svg` is its transparent cut. `aguagdl_favicon/` holds the favicon set; `web/` serves `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `android-chrome-*` and the manifest. Use `android-chrome-512x512.png` for profile photos. Never serve a 1 MB raster on the web.
