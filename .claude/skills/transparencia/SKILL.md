# Transparencia

Arguments: the job, such as a request id (F33, P2), «appeal <folio>», an export, screenshot or answer just sent, or «what's due».

The process lives in `research/transparency/` (gitignored, so it exists only in the main checkout, not in worktrees). This skill gives the order of the steps and says which file governs each one; it lives in a public repo, so case strategy stays in those files (see «Commits»). Read those files; don't work from memory or from this page. A filing that goes out wrong costs weeks and can't be taken back, so checking comes before speed and the three single-lens reviews stay the rule. The one exception is the last day: lateness has no cure (art. 98.1.I, l. 2638), and the garante «subsanará las deficiencias del recurso interpuesto» (96.5, l. 2624), which in our reading covers form, not new contents or a misquote. When prudent day 15 falls within the next two business days, today not counted (he files by hand on a PNT that often fails), give Ricardo the last text that passed `check.sh`, its ledger and a law-lens check of dates and grounds, with a dodge lens (DRAFTING-GUIDE «Final test» 1–7) run beside it in parallel, since it costs no time and finds exits the law lens doesn't read for; say what wasn't reviewed, and let him decide (lessons-learned §36). That route writes no reviewed marker, so the card's copy buttons stay locked: say so, and never tell him to copy from the card (18 Nov: the to-do page said «pega el texto de la tarjeta R12» and he found it locked). When that day 15 counts from an assumed date (the oficio's), ask him for the folio's «Seguimiento» screenshot before you start: on 18 Nov R12's assumed last day moved to six days later, and the full route would have fit.

## Start

1. **Effort.** Requests, appeals and legal analysis run at high reasoning effort or above, and so does every subagent you start for them (pass `effort: "high"`). If Ricardo has said the session runs lower, tell him before you draft. If you can't tell, don't guess.
2. **Read** `AGENT-CONTEXT.md`: «Where things stand», «Rules», «Facts settled against primary sources» and «Open findings», plus the row of «Where things are» that you need. Don't load `combined/`, `evidence/sources.json` or the source memos whole.
3. **Status comes from the log.** Take each folio's status from section D of `subsequent-requests.md` and the newest `mis_solicitudes-*.xls`, never from AGENT-CONTEXT, a README or STATE.md. PNT fields (acuse addressee, «fecha en que se notificó», due dates) are claims to check.
4. **Deadlines.** Run `python3 ~/agua-gdl-notes/scripts/plazos.py` and `plazos.py --check` together and read both uncut: a «what's due» from one of them misses windows (lessons-learned §35). Compute every date in Python. Write the legal date beside the PNT date, because the PNT counts rest days as working days (AGENT-CONTEXT rule 15). Jalisco answer terms are `start` + 8 business days (art. 84.1, l. 2387) with the PNT's date kept as `pnt_due`. Count our windows from the earlier date, and file a no-answer queja only after the later one.
5. **Someone may be editing.** Run `git -C ~/agua-gdl/research status --porcelain` and check the file's modification time right before you write to it. Before you take a new F or R number, read the other sessions' uncommitted cards (`git -C ~/agua-gdl/research diff -- transparency/subsequent-requests.md`) and tell every transparency session in `ListAgents` the range you take.

**Every legal citation comes from the law.** Before you write an article number, read it in `verification-2026-11-03/captures/07-legal-status/ltaipejm-180823.txt` (or the federal text beside it) this session, and give its line. A guide, a memo, AGENT-CONTEXT or an agent report is a lead for which article to read, never the source. On 6 Nov a dismissal rule (99.1.IV, l. 2654) and the wrong-body referral (81.3, l. 2346) were copied into the guide that way, as the rules for delivery and for referral windows, and were misapplied. Quote a paragraph or fracción whole, or mark the cut with «…», and never join pieces of two articles into one quote. A note in a guide, the register or `plazos.json` gets the fit a filed text gets: one 93.1 fracción per move, each read beside what the oficio did (lessons-learned §35).

**Every claim names its source.** This covers drafts, decisions, deadlines and your messages to Ricardo, not only filed texts. Give each fact the file and line, page or cell it came from, read this session; otherwise mark it «not checked». «From the 4 Nov review» is not a source. A subagent's report, a memo's «verified» or a status file is a lead until you read the source yourself, and so are a conversation summary and your memory of a source after a context reset: re-read the source before you write a note, a register row or a guide sentence from them (lessons-learned §35). A finding of your own review is a claim too: it gets a ledger row, with what you read, before you tell Ricardo or write it into a guide (lessons-learned §25).

**A subagent's report is checked by code, then by you.** Every agent you start writes its claims to a CSV (`id,claim,quote,file,line,how_read,verdict`: the exact source words and the line where they start) and runs `python3 check_claims.py <csv>` before it reports; one that writes a `.md` under `transparency/` also runs `check_cites.py` on it. Give each agent its own scratch subfolder, and tell it to download nothing: it asks you, and you ask Ricardo. Fetching a PDF, spreadsheet or zip link is a download, since the tool saves the file (18 Nov: a search agent's fetch saved the CEA's 7 MB fallo): the agent lists such links with what the page says about them and doesn't open them. You run the checks again yourself, then read by hand the meaning of every row that reaches a text or Ricardo. A finding with no quote doesn't reach him (18 Nov: «do not trust the agents»). When `check_claims.py` prints ALSO, the same words sit twice in the file (a resolution repeats an area's oficio): pin the area's own oficio, not the resolution's extract (lessons-learned §40).
Archiving a news page with curl is a download too: on 18 Nov five were archived unasked, and Ricardo was told afterwards (lessons-learned §45).

**Official statements are claims to test, never facts.** This covers what a politician, a government body, its press release or its oficio says, including what it told the CEDHJ and what an answer says it delivered. For each one:
- register each factual assertion an answer makes (areas searched, inexistence, «se adjunta», a link, competence, a figure, «se informará») in `answer-statements-<date>.md` with its test: attachment present, link opens the record (87.2), areas the right ones, figure against other answers; verdict holds, contradicted (counter-document pinned), unsupported or not checked;
- set it against the documents and against what other officials said (`official-contradictions.md`); a bulletin's lead can overstate its own quotes, so read the official's quoted words before you register the lead's figure as theirs (lessons-learned §40);
- write down the question it raises;
- turn the strongest questions into a new request. An appeal can't add contents to the request (98.1.VIII, l. 2647, makes it improcedente for them), so a question raised after filing never goes into an appeal.

The reasons are in DRAFTING-GUIDE «The stance» and AGENT-CONTEXT rule 7.

## Answers

Follow DRAFTING-GUIDE «After an answer arrives» for each folio. The points that are easy to miss:

- **Overlap groups first.** Answers that cover the same records are read together (`verification-2026-11-03/09-answer-readiness.md`, a lead; its 6 Nov table covers the newer folios). Before you decide an appeal that waits on an answer, list its group from the readiness note and the answers plan, say which of those answers are in hand, and read every one in hand beside the appeal: write down what each gives the appeal (an official document where its proof of existence is press) and what it gives the other side. A decision made on one answer of a group says so.
- **A literal reading of a label.** DRAFTING-GUIDE «After an answer arrives» says which requests invite one. When the answer denies the label but describes the activity, quote both and appeal under 93.1.X (l. 2579; DRAFTING-GUIDE «Other moves in an answer»).
- **What counts as delivery.** Pointing to published information counts only if it gives «la fuente, el lugar y la forma» (art. 87.2, l. 2454) and the link opens the record asked for. A general portal, or another of our own pending folios, doesn't. Open every link an answer cites, and when it leads to a search, run it: record what it found and the search it took (dates, fields), so whether the answer said where and how to find the record can be judged. A file it serves is a download: Ricardo's OK first.
- **Downloads.** Ask Ricardo once per batch, listing each folio's files and sizes. His OK covers that list. If he prefers, he sends the files himself. Use `python3 download_responses.py <export>`, which fetches with curl and refuses an empty zip. List each zip's entries and tell him how many answers actually came with a file. A WebFetch of a file URL saves the file, so it is a download too: check a file's existence and size with `curl -I`, and tell every subagent «no WebFetch on a file URL» (lessons-learned §47). Before a law or reglamento goes on the list, find the version in force from the body's own list of applicable rules and the Periódico Oficial's index: a web search's copy may be superseded (19 Nov: a 2017 SEMADET Reglamento replaced in 2021). A text on disk that isn't in force goes in `answers/superseded-texts.json`, and `check_register.py` fails a row pinned to it that doesn't say so.
- **Read the whole resolution, images included.** A resolution can paste the area's answer as an image with no text layer, so the text extraction skips the paragraph that says who searched and where. Open every page; transcribe such a paragraph from the page into `combined/<folio>.txt`, marked as a transcription, and register the source as read from the image. A chart or table pasted as an image is transcribed the same way, value by value, with the digits the print leaves uncertain listed.
- **Set the resolution against itself and against the request.** Which body it names as answering and which areas it asked, against the body and areas the request named; whether it answers point by point; every attachment its resolutivos order delivered (by the number and date on the attachment's own heading, which can differ from the resolution's), and every one an area's oficio promises («adjunto», «anexo», «para mejor proveer», a format such as Excel), against the files that arrived, with the foot of the last page read for a cut sentence; each delivered table's columns against the redactions the request allowed, on the page image; whether it reads the bulletin or news item the request started from as the thing asked for; and each norm or criterio it cites, read whole (step 2 of «Drafting»). DRAFTING-GUIDE «After an answer arrives» item 4 has the searches. INAI's criterios are archived in `verification-2026-11-03/captures/07-legal-status/inai-criterios-compilacion.txt`.
- **Before you report something missing,** prove the absence. A parse's count is reconciled with the total the document announces and with a plain `grep -c` of one field, and you read a sample of what the pattern dropped. A column, attachment or act is checked on the page image, since a black bar, a scan with no text layer or a paragraph under another heading reads as absence in text. A body called idle is searched for in Ricardo's mailbox, spam and trash included, by name and acronym: a separate body that receives a referral may admit it by email with no PNT folio, and each such admission opens its own `plazos.json` entry, even when they share dates, and a line in `pnt-subjects.md`. Name our request by its id in that entry's `what` and put its folio in `basis`: `check_export.py` reads the first folio in `what` as the entry's own. Write «referred» only where an oficio or the export shows a referral; an area that says another body runs the operation, with no referral, has referred nothing (DRAFTING-GUIDE «Legal traps»). Name exactly what an act lacks («no new Comité act»), not the nearest category («no harm test») (lessons-learned §35).
- **«Not on disk» is a claim too.** Before writing it, search by the document's number and by the folio (`refs.py find`, `acuses/`, `combined/`, the register itself) and write «searched <day> <Mon>: <what was run>» in the row; `check_register.py`, run by `check.sh`, fails a row without it and a row whose number shows on a document already on disk. Its known gaps (other phrasings, a claim with no number, documents outside the research tree, bodies with no archived Reglamento) are listed in its docstring and in lessons-learned §47: search those by hand. On 18 Nov four «not on disk» entries were wrong.
- **An inexistence with no Comité ruling** is scored `none`, with a note on the reason the answer gives for having no ruling; DRAFTING-GUIDE «When they declare inexistence without the Comité» says how to read it.
- **Score every item asked** in `answers/scores-<date>.csv` (date, body, verdict, move, the page or link, properly addressed or not), and take every count from `python3 score_answers.py`. The verdicts are delivered, partial, none, inexistent-ruled (a complete ruling under 86-Bis.3, l. 2425–2437, isn't a defeat), reserved (it confirms a file exists) and delivered-unusable (with a note). A rescore is a new row with a later date; only the latest counts. After each batch, record `--moves` (the routes each body used) in the round's README. No appeal decision, «No» included, before every item the request asked has a row: an item the answer transcribes or names and then doesn't answer is `none` with move `omision`. `score_answers.py --check` fails an open `rr-` window whose folio has no rows and isn't in `answers/unscored-baseline.json`, which may only shrink (lessons-learned §36). A body with no PNT folio of its own (a referral it admitted by email) is scored under the referring folio with its items prefixed by the body («PCyB 1»): only the latest row per folio and item counts, so a bare «1» would replace the first body's row.
- **Each lawful move documents evasion, not a cover-up.** Record it as «X answered with Y», with the oficio. The narrative's count is redone from properly addressed rows only.
- **Deadlines the same day.** Mark the answer entry `answered`. Open the appeal entry `rr-<x>` with the folio in `what`, starting at the oficio date marked as assumed; replace that date when the «Seguimiento» screenshot arrives. The oficio's date is only the safe count: it never goes into a text or the Registro as the notification date. Until the screenshot arrives, write in `basis` the date the answer reached Ricardo (the email's time) and the file's creation date (`pdfinfo`): a later date can move day 15 later, and the earlier one stays the count we file by (lessons-learned §35). Each act gets its own entry: `rr-<x>` the answer, `rr-<x>-ref` a referral, `rr-<x>-sin-respuesta` no answer. A referral is appealed as a declaration of incompetence (art. 93.1.XI, l. 2580), 15 days from its own notification (95.1.I, l. 2597). For a referral, or an answer that declines competence, also record the date the body says it received the request and the business days it took, computed in Python (DRAFTING-GUIDE «Legal traps»).
- **Extensions.** Record an extension as `extended_to`, with its Comité resolution and the date it was notified to Ricardo, never by changing `days`. Without both, it isn't a new due date: the request is unanswered. Either way our window keeps counting from the original end.
- **Before you write or change a register entry**, run the lookup of «Drafting» step 1 on its places, bodies and contract numbers, and read every `narrative-gaps-*.md`: what they hold goes in the entry, or the entry says why not. `validate_three_drafts.py` fails an entry about works at a place whose contracts an archived works list holds and the entry doesn't name (lessons-learned §30).
- **Amounts.** Before calling a difference between two costs an overrun, test it against × 1.16 (IVA), then read both columns' headings: when both say «IVA INCLUIDO», a × 1.16 match is unresolved, not IVA, until the contract or finiquito shows it (lessons-learned §32).
- **When an answer changes a conclusion**, update the narrative assessment, `public-claims-vs-responses.md` and `official-contradictions.md`. Then take each folio through «Appeals» and record the decision in the «Decisión por folio» table.
- **Carry what a batch teaches forward the same day.** Each move a batch shows that the guide didn't foresee goes into DRAFTING-GUIDE («Close their exits», «Routes a well-advised official would use next» or «After an answer arrives») and lessons-learned. Then read every unfiled draft for the same opening and rewrite it; a rewritten text loses its reviewed marker and goes through the three lenses again. List, as proposals for Ricardo, the follow-up requests the answers make possible: a record an answer describes but didn't deliver (an order, a minuta, an acta, a log), asked by its own number from the body that holds it, each checked against section D first.

## Drafting (requests and appeals)

1. **Don't research or file twice.** Run `python3 evidence/refs.py find "<term>"`, read AGENT-CONTEXT «Already investigated» and the topic's `verification-*/README.md`, and check section D for a pending request on the same records. Search the addressee's own published lists and ask by its numbers, never by another body's oficio (DRAFTING-GUIDE «Before drafting» 8). Rewriting an unfiled draft, or relying on a pending follow-up, beats adding a new request. A new request has to name what it settles that nothing pending does. Check whether an appeal would beat a new request, since a new request doesn't keep an appeal's deadline. When a new request and a pending or drafted appeal ask the same records, split them by date and pin the appeal's cut-off to a date the request's own period supports (read the sentence that asks those records, not the request as a whole), not «la fecha de respuesta». A date past that period widens the request (98.1.VIII, l. 2647–2648). Then read the request's other items: a later report or a concentrado spanning both periods can still moot the appeal, and the body can attach it to its answer in the appeal whether or not the request is filed. A delivery inside the appeal is 99.1.IV (l. 2654–2656), which needs our conformity; 99.1.V (l. 2657–2658) and LGTAIP 159.III (`lgtaip-diputados.txt` l. 2654–2655) don't. Write «shouldn't moot», never «can't», and record what the split leaves unasked (lessons-learned §39). Take an earlier request's period from what was filed (the export's «Descripción» or the answer's transcription), never from its register draft, which keeps the placeholder date: `check_claims.py` fails a placeholder quote pinned in the draft of a request that has a folio (lessons-learned §43).
   Read the unfiled drafts as well as section D (P-ids, and register sections with no folio): on 18 Nov P3, unfiled, asked F51's records with the opposite redaction rule (lessons-learned §45).
2. **Read every source yourself.** For a scanned document, compare quotes with the page image, not the OCR. Before you state what an act decided (a fallo, a resolution, an acta), read its operative part: a heading can repeat the name of an earlier step (18 Nov: every page of the CEA's pipa fallo says «…DECLARADO DESIERTO», and the fallo awards three pipas; lessons-learned §44). Get line, page and cell pins from code (`grep -n`, `awk '{print NR}'`, a parse), never by estimating them, and never with `nl`, which skips blank lines (lessons-learned §39). Set every paraphrase of a law beside the whole fracción or paragraph, each one the sentence names and any neighbour that qualifies it (90.1.II, l. 2526–2528, lets the body choose the informe específico that 90.1.I, l. 2523–2525, says can't be imposed), and quote it whole in its ledger row: a row that checks only the number passes a dropped condition, or a dropped «debe» (`wording.name_slips`). Name a body by its creating law, not by the PNT catalogue. Read and archive every norm an oficio gives as its basis (a reglamento, a criterio, another law) before you answer it; one you couldn't read is «not checked». In a sweep, search the literal accented word, never an accented letter inside a bracket expression (in this shell's C locale `[oó]` matches the unaccented letter and never the accented one), and run the pattern on a known hit before you trust a zero.
3. **Ledger.** Give every claim a row in `verification-<date>-<topic>/sources-for-every-claim.md` with the source, its pin, how you read it and the verdict, pinned to the text by its hash. `validate_three_drafts.py` fails a text without one. `check_cites.py` reads any number followed by «(l. N)» as a law pin and counts every unpinned article, in the ledger and in an appeal's internal note alike: give each law cite its line, and call an oficio's lines «renglones». It also reads «p. N, l. N» as an article pin: write another source's page and line as «p. N, text line N». A «quote» in the same clause as a law pin is tested as the law's words, even across a table cell or row: separate our words or an oficio's from the pin with «;», «:» or a full stop.
   Write the ledger while you draft, not after: a sentence with no source to put in its row comes out of the text. Write the hash pin last, once `python3 measure_cards.py --draft <file>` passes and the pin is the validator's only failure: every rule on the text runs before the pin check, so an earlier pin gets written twice.
   Text inserted into a shared file moves the pins of every ledger below it: `check.sh` runs `check_claims.py` on every ledger CSV, and `python3 check_claims.py --repin <csv>` moves a pin to the one line where its quote now starts (18 Nov: sections H and J moved twelve pins of two other ledgers; lessons-learned §45).
4. **Name the type and its content.** Write each item as «el [tipo] o los documentos en que consten [contenido]», so a literal reading of the label doesn't close it; the validator fails numbered items without it and lists prose ones (DRAFTING-GUIDE «Name the type and its content»). A bulletin, news item or oficio the request starts from is cited, with its date and URL, as context only, never as the thing asked for or a condition the records must match (DRAFTING-GUIDE «Cite the anchor as context, never as the object»).
5. **Write the text** into its home file: F in `subsequent-requests.md`, P in `drafts-three-priority-requests-2026-11-01.md`, R in `recursos-de-revision-2026-11-02.md`. `validate_three_drafts.py` fails a text over 3,900 characters (the PNT box takes 4,000); report the count. A request card is measured with the standard clause appended (item «5. Unless…» of `subsequent-requests.md`, with the filing date in place of the placeholder), an appeal alone: measure as the validator does, never the text alone: `python3 measure_cards.py --draft <file>` on a scratch draft (each `### ID` section's `>` lines, joined as the builder joins them, plus the validator's wording checks), `python3 measure_cards.py <ids>` on the built page. Never use a counter of your own (18 Nov: one that collapsed the builder's paragraph breaks showed three texts under the cap that were 1–12 characters over). Rebuild the page and run the validator as soon as the text is in its home file, before trimming for length: it lists the clauses every `now` card needs, and a clause cut to fit fails only at the end. Don't quote the other side's words where they read as its defence («se podrá generar» reads as figures not yet generated); cite the fact under them.
6. **Three single-lens reviews, in parallel:** read-only `verificador` agents at high effort, briefed from section D, each given the text and the ledger and told to assume mistakes exist. The lenses are (a) facts and quotes, letter for letter; (b) law and procedure, meaning grounds, custodian, `pnt-subjects.md` and dates; (c) the official trying to dodge (DRAFTING-GUIDE «Final test», 1–7). `verificador` doesn't load CLAUDE.md and is briefed for evidence, so the law lens's brief has to include DRAFTING-GUIDE «Legal traps» and the relevant rows of `legal-options.md`. Brief every lens with the room left under 3,900 (standard clause included) and the wording the validator holds verbatim (the closing «Para cada punto…» paragraph, «un total o resumen no sustituye»), so it doesn't propose what can't go in. Save each lens's report verbatim in the round folder as `lens-<x>-report.txt` (`.txt`, because `check_cites.py` reads every `.md` as our own claims). Check every finding against its source before you apply it. A fix that adds a claim, a ground or an ask goes back to the lens whose finding it answers (SendMessage to the same agent) before the round counts. Record which lens read which version: «the lenses confirmed it» is true only of the ones that read the final text. When the final text differs from what a lens read, even by a word, send that lens the final passage before the marker: it costs one message. Then write the reviewed marker (DRAFTING-GUIDE «Reviewed marker»).
   Give the lenses frozen copies of the texts in your scratch folder, not the live files. Their CSV rows quote the text they read, which the round then changes: after the fixes, run those rows against the frozen copies and write both counts (failing against the current files, passing against the copies) in the round's README (lessons-learned §45).
   A change queued by an earlier round is a proposal, not a decision. Before you apply it, write the alternatives beside it, the cut included (removing the passage it repairs), and ask the dodge lens which version the official would rather face. Brief the facts and dodge lenses to read every attachment whole for what it gives the other side, not only the lines the text quotes. When a lens proposes what a guide rule already asks for, it isn't optional: apply it, or record why not, and check every text that shares the clause.
   The lenses check that each article says what the text claims, not what opposing counsel spots at first glance, so read for that yourself with DRAFTING-GUIDE «Legal traps» (lessons-learned §24). `wording.legal_slips`, `wording.xi_unsupported`, `wording.comite_without_alternative` and `wording.inexistence_unnamed` fail the commonest slips; `wording.foreign_scope`, `wording.missing_works_numbers` and `wording.bulk_without_electronic` fail a request scoped by another body's oficio, one that leaves out the addressee's own contract numbers, and one that asks for source records without the electronic-delivery sentence. When one clause is copied into several texts (a merge, a template change), the merged texts go to fresh lenses («Legal traps» says what else to check).
   Two more reads are yours, because no lens does them (lessons-learned §25); do them before the lenses start, so their briefs carry what you found, and never report a lens's answer to either as your own read. Read each text against every filed or drafted appeal that shares a document or a garante, so an argument in one isn't the other body's defence. Then read it against the law and our own memos in `legal-research/` for what they give us that the text doesn't use. Record each such point in the round's README with whether it went in, or why not.
